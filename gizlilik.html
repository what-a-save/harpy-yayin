Gizlilik Politikasi / Privacy Policy

Son guncelleme: 1 Kasim 2026

Harpy Yayin yalnizca Harpy Crow kanalinin sahibi tarafindan, kendi hesaplari icin kullanilan kisisel bir aractir.

  • Arac, YouTube, Instagram ve TikTok'un resmi API'leri uzerinden yalnizca sahibinin yetki verdigi hesaplara video yukler.
  • Erisim anahtarlari yalnizca sahibinin kendi bilgisayarinda saklanir; hicbir sunucuya gonderilmez, ucuncu kisilerle paylasilmaz, satilmaz.
  • Baska kullanicilardan hicbir kisisel veri toplanmaz.
  • Kullanilan TikTok verisi: temel profil bilgisi (gorunen ad) ve video yukleme izni. Bu veriler sadece videoyu sahibinin TikTok gelen kutusuna gondermek icin kullanilir.
  • Yetki istenildigi an ilgili platformun ayarlarindan geri alinabilir.

Harpy Yayin is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Harpy Crow channel for their own accounts. It uploads videos only to accounts the owner has authorized via the official YouTube, Instagram and TikTok APIs. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. No personal data is collected from anyone else. TikTok data used: basic profile info (display name) and the video upload permission, used solely to send the owner's videos to their own TikTok inbox. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings.
